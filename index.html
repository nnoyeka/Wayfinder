      About Wayfinder

      Wayfinder brings together live data from free public APIs:

      • Open-Meteo for city locations and weather forecasts
      • World Bank API for country facts
      • Nager.Date for public holidays
      • Frankfurter for currency exchange rates
      • Flagpedia (flagcdn) for country flags
